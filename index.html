01.

About

I'm a mechanical engineer working on planning and control for connected and automated vehicles, vehicle energy efficiency, and transportation electrification. From 2024 to 2026 I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at UC Davis, working with the AHMCT Research Center and CORE Lab, where I led Caltrans research on mobile DC fast charging and commissioned a dSPACE SCALEXIO HIL/SIL environment for validating vehicle control algorithms.

My work runs from models to hardware: predictive and optimal control (MPC, dynamic programming) for eco-driving, a deep bilinear Koopman vehicle model for real-time MPC, deep-learning and physics-informed energy and emissions models, and chassis-dynamometer testing of autonomous electric vehicles. I work mainly in Python, C++, MATLAB, ROS, CARLA, and NREL FASTSim.

I hold a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Western Michigan University, an M.Sc. in Automotive Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology, and a B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Semnan University. Across academia and industry, I've been drawn to the same thing: the interplay between rigor and beauty — where mathematical structure produces systems that are not only correct, but elegant.

My focus is closing the gap between research and deployment — models that survive contact with real fleets, real roads, and real weather.
